I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I work with Dr. Sharifa Sultana in the Illinois Responsible AI (RAI) Group. My research spans Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Human-AI Interaction (HAI), and Information and Communication Technologies for Development (ICTD). In my work, I study how people in informal and resource-constrained settings use computing tools, what challenges they face, and how we can design better tools in a participatory manner to empower them. My work has been published in HCI and NLP venues such as CHI, DIS, ICTD, COMPASS, and ACL.

Before UIUC, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). I was also a lecturer at BRAC University, Bangladesh.
